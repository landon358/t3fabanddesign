About the shop

A small shop on the west side

T3 Design & Fab is a welding and metal fabrication shop at 1255 Leonard Street NW in Grand Rapids. It is two of us. Tom runs the business side. His son Thomas does the welding and the fabrication, and holds a manufacturing engineering degree from Grand Rapids Community College. We have been at this since 2004.

We are not a big operation and we do not want to be. We have a small shop with very good equipment, and we have put most of what we make back into it — the wheel machine, the rust repair tooling, the service truck. When you call, you talk to the person doing the work.

We also fix up bikes and give some of them away to kids who need one. That is not a marketing thing. It is just what happens when a bike shows up and it can be saved.
